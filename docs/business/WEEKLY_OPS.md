# Weekly Operating Plan — template + North Star Metrics

Starts being used from `GO_TO_MARKET.md` Day 14 onward (before that, Day
0–14 is a fixed one-time launch sequence, not a recurring weekly cycle).
Copy the template below into a dated entry each week once live; keep entries
appended, don't overwrite — this becomes the operational history alongside
`experiments/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md`.

## Why six sections

Technical development and the business have to move in the same week, not
in separate tracks that only meet at milestones. Each week answers all six:

- **BUILD** — what got technically improved (pipeline, QA, automation)
- **CREATE** — what content got produced
- **PUBLISH** — what actually went out, where, on what cadence
- **GROW** — what growth experiment(s) ran, with success/kill criteria
- **MONETIZE** — what moved (or was tested) toward revenue
- **MEASURE** — the numbers, against the North Star set below

A week that's all BUILD and no PUBLISH/GROW is a warning sign at MVP stage —
per `CLAUDE.md` §1, the goal is launch and learning speed, not pipeline
polish for its own sake.

```
  affiliate links live, actual revenue if any]

**MEASURE**
- [North Star Metrics snapshot — see below]

**Next week's single biggest priority:**
- [one line — forces prioritization instead of a flat task list]
```

## North Star Metrics (tracked every week from Day 14 onward)

| Metric | Why it's north star, not vanity |
|---|---|
| Days to Launch | Forces urgency pre-launch; becomes N/A once launched |
| Days to First Revenue | The real validation milestone, not follower count |
| Cost per Publishable Asset | `COSTS.md` funnel metric — controls whether volume is affordable |
| Content Acceptance Rate | Health of the identity/QA pipeline, not just output volume |
| Followers Growth Rate | Trend, not absolute number — a flat trend matters more than a high count |
| Engagement Rate | Signals content-pillar fit, feeds `GO_TO_MARKET.md` hypothesis testing |
| Conversion Rate | Followers → paying — the dominant lever in `REVENUE_MODEL.md` |
| Monthly Revenue | Actual, not projected |
| Monthly Profit | Revenue minus everything in `COSTS.md` — the number that actually matters |
| Hours of Human Work / Week | The automation thesis only holds if this stays low as volume grows — track it honestly |

The business objective, restated plainly (`CLAUDE.md` §1): **a highly
profitable business with the minimum possible human intervention** — not the
most technically sophisticated pipeline. If a week's BUILD work doesn't
eventually move one of these numbers, question whether it was the right
priority (ROI framing, `CLAUDE.md` §5).
